Options

This shortens the story scenes.

  • Childhood cutscenes and forced conversations are skipped. Items and songs are still given.
  • From the Temple of Time on, scenes are only shortened.
  • Boss intros and defeats are kept.

Still in development: some scenes may not be skipped yet.

This adds spoiler.txt to the mod folder.

It lists where every item went. Turn it off if you don't want to be tempted.

Key items

This decides where key items can appear.

'Vanilla': every key item stays in its original location.

'Any chest': key items are shuffled among chests. With Tokensanity or Shuffle Songs turned on, they can also be on those Gold Skulltulas or song locations.

'Any chest' also shuffles the six Great Fairy rewards (magic, Din's Fire, Farore's Wind, Nayru's Love, double magic and double defense), as OoTR does. Each fountain can give any item. The fairies are free, and a second visit only heals you.

'Any chest' also shuffles the NPC gifts, as OoTR does: Darunia, Zelda, Malon, the Skull Kid, the Light and Fire Arrows, the rolling Goron, King Zora, Anju, the lost dog, the man on the roof, the Big Poes and Ruto's Letter. Each can give any item.

It also shuffles the minigame prizes and the Gold Skulltula house rewards: the galleries, the Lost Woods target, Bombchu Bowling, fishing, the lab dive, the Zora diving game, the Skull Kid's ocarina game, the frogs, horseback archery, Dampe and Talon. The Golden Scale always comes after the Silver one in the logic, and excluding one scale excludes both.

These chests never get a key item in this version:

  • The Market Treasure Chest Game chests
  • Chests that hold small keys, maps or compasses

Shuffle

This shuffles the Gold Skulltula Tokens.

'Off': every Gold Skulltula gives its own token.

'Dungeons Only': the Gold Skulltulas inside dungeons can drop any item, and their tokens are spread among chests and other Skulltulas.

'Overworld Only': the same, for the Gold Skulltulas outside dungeons.

'All Tokens': every Gold Skulltula.

This restricts where songs can appear.

'Off': every song is taught where it normally is.

'Song Locations': the 12 songs are shuffled among the places that normally teach a song:

  • Impa, Saria and Malon
  • The Royal Family's Tomb and the windmill
  • The Ocarina of Time
  • Sheik's songs

The scenes still name their original song.

'Anywhere': songs can be in any chest or song location, never on a Gold Skulltula. Song locations can give any item except Gold Skulltula tokens. Needs key items set to 'Any chest'.

'Dungeon Rewards': songs appear only at the ends of dungeons:

  • The 8 boss Heart Containers
  • Impa and Sheik in the Ice Cavern
  • The Gerudo Training Ground's Ice Arrows chest and the Well's Lens of Truth chest

The other song locations can give any item. Needs key items set to 'Any chest'; Lens of Truth and Ice Arrows cannot be excluded.

This shuffles the Spiritual Stones and the Medallions.

'Dungeon Reward Locations': they are shuffled among the places that normally give one (the bosses and Rauru). The Door of Time and the bridge still open when you beat the bosses, as in the remake.

'Vanilla': each boss gives its usual reward.

What makes the rainbow bridge to Ganon's Castle appear.

'Vanilla': as in the remake, after Zelda gives you the Light Arrows (Shadow and Spirit Temples beaten).

'Medallions': as soon as you own the number of medallions chosen below, wherever they came from.

With 'Random Number' on, the count is random too (0 to 6). The spoiler log lists the trials to clear.

This shuffles the Fairy Ocarina and the Ocarina of Time.

Saria's gift on the bridge and Zelda's gift at the castle moat can give any item, and the ocarinas can be anywhere. Needs key items set to 'Any chest'.

This shuffles the Gerudo Membership Card.

In this version the card has no use: the Gerudo let you through once the carpenters are rescued. The card can be anywhere, and its place only gives minor items (rupees, ammo...). Needs key items set to 'Any chest'.

This shuffles what Medigoron in Goron City and the carpet salesman in the Haunted Wasteland sell.

Each sells one item, only once. The Giant's Knife and the salesman's Bombchus can be anywhere. Needs key items set to 'Any chest'.

Logic

Every generated seed can be finished without glitches.

It uses OoTR's glitchless logic, plus a few rules where the remake is stricter (for example, the Deku Tree's upper floor needs a ranged item).

Install

  1. Unzip the downloaded file. It contains a Rando_<seed> folder.
  2. Copy that folder into load/0400486024DEC000/ inside Lichii's data folder (right-click the game in Lichii to open its mods folder, if offered).
  3. Keep only one Rando_* mod enabled at a time.
  4. Start a new game. An existing save does not pick up the changes.

The spoiler log (spoiler.txt) inside the folder lists where every item went.

Source code and known limitations: github.com/WekarXD/OOT-Remake-Randomizer. No game files are included.
